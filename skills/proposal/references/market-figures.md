## Rates observed on Upwork (2,254 hourly AI postings, May 2026)

p25 $19 · **median $27.50** · p75 $40 · p90 $60. Only 3.5% budget $75/hr+.
Fixed: median budget **$170**, p75 $800, p90 $2,600.

Off-platform for the same skills: AI agents $80–250/hr · LiveKit/WebRTC $120–220/hr ·
LLMOps gateway $120–250/hr · Claude Code specialists $100–300/hr. **Expect a 30–60%
haircut on Upwork.**

## Connects

$0.15 flat · 10 free/month Basic · 6 per standard proposal · 36/month earnable free via
the proposal-activity reward · Identity Verified costs 35 · Plus is $19.99/mo for 100.

At 2% reply: ~300 Connects ($45) per reply, ~1,200 ($180) per contract.

## Badges

Available Now (free toggle) **+15–25%** · Top Rated Plus +25–40% · Expert-Vetted +60–90% ·
Top Rated +5–15% · Rising Talent +3–8% · Skill Certification ~0% (dead program).

Top Rated: JSS ≥90% for 13 of last 16 weeks, ≥$1,000 earned across multiple clients, first
project 90+ days ago, 100% profile, Identity Verified.

## Known weaknesses in this data

1. No public per-skill Upwork job counts exist. Only Upwatcher's 5,536-posting AI-category
   scrape is a real count.
2. Rate bands for gateway, realtime, OAuth brokering and rescue come from **off-platform**
   markets.
3. The Claude Code **+938%** figure is **Fiverr search volume**, not Upwork hiring, not
   revenue.
4. GigRadar's reply rates are **agency-side automated outbound** across 133,872 proposals.
   The relative ordering of categories is reliable; the absolute percentages are not one
   careful human's percentages.
5. "Vibe coding cleanup" as a paying Upwork category is extrapolated from mechanism
   (CVE counts, 45% vulnerability rate, 63% non-developer users), not measured posting
   volume.

When citing any of these to a client, cite the mechanism, not the percentage.
